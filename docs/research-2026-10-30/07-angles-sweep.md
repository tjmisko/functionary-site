## 2. Founder doubt and hedges (do not let copy overclaim)

- **The thesis may be wrong:** "now maybe nothing will ever go wrong in an agent + adversarial review loop" / "or maybe the mess is just fine and these costs are trivial compared to benefits" / "that seems to be what most people think and they may well be right" (WFI msgs 5–7). Copy should argue for the exposed buyer, not claim the mainstream is wrong.
- **"at least in theory"** (msg 28) follows the whole knowledge-base/provenance/agents-use-it paragraph. Every row-9 and row-15 claim is aspirational until built.
- **"slightly ocean-boily"** (msg 29): building the per-block custom agents. Also "getting the whole execution environment properly written is fairly hard and subtle" (msg 30). Copy must not imply a large, mature block catalog. The production catalog is Source, Summarize, Search, HumanReview, Judge, Write, TableSource plus three interim Transforms (`lib.rs:441-600`).
- **"more or less exactly"** (msg 17): the founder's own hedge on canvas-equals-execution.
- **Segmentation doubt:** "A general model given a lot of context will probably produce low-quality output on average; the folds into the Extract class (S17) are hypotheses until tested" (Decisions Log, "Registered, not ruled").
- **Atomize:** "prototype, no v1 promise" (Decisions Log; F103.2).
- **Coverage:** "You cannot honestly promise 'every available source.'" (exhaustive-coverage-search.md).
- **Review of process deferred:** "Review gates might also want to review what was done in addition to what the output was… defer for now." (U6, 2026-10-09). "Review on a block" is an idea (S28).
- **Disliked names:** Replicate ("The user dislikes the name **Replicate**"); Model schema presets ("the user did not like the presets as proposed", S47).
- **Not in v1:** custom blocks (P9; open-questions §14), export (D49), local inner-agent models (F99.17 blocked), automated Redact (handoff §8).

## 4. Vocabulary

**Rules that still hold** (from the 2026-06-01 Trust Thesis §4 and site memory):
- Public spine words: **delegation**, **trust**. In 2026-10 "trust" mostly attaches to components ("trusted blocks", "untrusted coding agent", P1 and the 2026-09-29 feedback).
- Never publish: bureaucracy, principal-agent, agency cost, moral hazard, adverse selection, context engineering, enframing, Gestell, ready-to-hand. Note that D56 also rejected bureaucracy-themed construct names.
- Never: harden, hardening, ossify, calcify. Use "the process learns" or "stabilize" (Archetypes).
- No spider or web metaphor. The 2026-09-12 rebrand (`ops/planning/2026-09-12 - Rebrand Arachne to Functionary.md`) makes the Arachne metaphor moot.
- Avoid "Freestyle" (rejected 2026-06-19), "discretion" as a mechanism term, "Agent Sketch", "Graduate", "superblock", "void block" and "Document/Record as the two payloads".
- For the Associate path, avoid "pin", "versioning", "approval" and "roles" (Archetypes: "They never see the word 'pin'"). "Hyperformalism" is an internal anti-pattern word, not copy.

**New 2026-10 vocabulary usable in copy** (glossary, `ops/planning/backend-ground-truth/glossary.md:163-224`):
- **Kinds (seven):** Source (ingress), Block (a delegable unit of agent judgement; every Block calls a model), Agent (the sketch agent), Transform, Jig, Gate, Egress (D135).
- **Verbs:** 33, in the palette for non-programmers. The session handoff: "the verb set must stay small enough for non-programmers." Blocks: Summarize, Search, Judge, Model, Quote, Compare, Draft. Gates: Review, Check, Declassify, Redact. Egress: Write, Notify, Apply. Transforms include Aggregate, Join, Reshape and Format.
- **Modes:** "Modes", not "channels" (S10).
- **Procedures:** named compositions shown as one card that expands into visible steps (Best of N, Adversarial review, Consolidate, Classify and route, Recover from failure). The name collides with D56's rejected "Procedure" for Jig. It is now adopted for compositions and fine in copy.
- **Origin:** Sourced, Computed, Generated, Verified, Reviewed. Plain-language candidates: "from a source", "computed", "AI-written", "checked", "reviewed".
- **Receipts:** "every model call and tool call leaves a receipt".
- **Check** (quote, hash, schema, predicate), **Review**, **Declassify**, **Redact**, **Anchor**, **Quote**, **effort** presets, **Test / Run / Escalate**, **strict mode**, **Personal / Governed** tenants, **Associate / Automator**, **iterate → stabilize**, **progressive formalization**.
- Disliked or provisional: Replicate. "Model" is a provisional name (D106).
